3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account management: To create, authenticate, and maintain your student account.
  • Content delivery: To provide tailored secondary & preparatory exam questions, derivations, formulas, diagrams, and multilingual explanations for your selected grade (Grades 9–12) and stream (Natural or Social Science).
  • Payment processing: To verify subscription upgrades via payment receipt upload and activate offline content packages.
  • Progress tracking: To display your personal exam history, accuracy percentages, speed pacing, and daily study streaks.
  • Notifications: To send daily study reminders, streak alerts, and curriculum update announcements (only if you have granted notification permission).
  • Analytics: To understand how students navigate curriculum topics, improve explanations, and resolve performance bottlenecks.
  • Security: To detect and prevent unauthorized access, content scraping, or tampering using Google Play Integrity API.
  • Advertising: To display banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open advertisements via Google AdMob to keep the application widely accessible.

5. Data Storage and Security

Where your data is stored

  • On your device: Account profile, quiz progress, downloaded question packages — stored in an encrypted local database (Android Room) secured by the Android Keystore.
  • Google Firebase servers: Account profile, exam sync data, crash logs, and analytics. Firebase servers are located in secure Google Cloud facilities.
  • Cloudflare infrastructure: Payment receipt screenshots and encrypted curriculum content packages delivered via Cloudflare's high-speed global CDN.

How we protect your data

  • All network communication uses HTTPS (TLS). Cleartext HTTP is explicitly blocked.
  • Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed — never stored in plain text.
  • Downloaded curriculum packages are AES-encrypted and validated with SHA-256 hash checksums.
  • Crash reports are anonymized — phone numbers, names, and passwords are never included in crash logs.
  • We do not enable Android ADB backup (android:allowBackup="false"), preventing database extraction via unauthorized USB debugging.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained for as long as you maintain an active account.
  • Exam history: Retained until you delete your account or manually purge data in the app's Storage Manager.
  • Payment records: Retained for a minimum of 2 years for financial audit and subscription verification purposes.
  • Crash logs: Retained for 90 days in Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Analytics data: Retained as configured in Firebase Analytics (default: 14 months).
  • Ad data: Managed by Google AdMob per their standard data retention policies.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Data Reset & Account Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data directly within the App: Open App → Preferences / Storage Manager → Delete Offline Data / Delete Account. This instantly clears your local question cache, exam history, and stored preferences.

Notifications

You can disable study reminders and streak notifications at any time through your device's settings (Settings → Apps → Triple E → Notifications) or inside the App's preferences sheet.

Advertising Preferences

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android system settings: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" (Android 12+) or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (older Android versions).

Data Access & Inquiries

To request a copy of your personal data, correct inaccurate details, or ask questions regarding our privacy policies, contact us at the email address below.

8. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support free access to educational materials for all students. Types of ads include:

  • Banner ads (displayed at the top or bottom of designated screens)
  • Interstitial ads (full-screen transitions shown between completed study sessions)
  • Rewarded video ads (optional, watched to unlock specific bonus mock exams)
  • App Open ads (shown when resuming the app; suppressed for first-time users)

Ads are never shown immediately upon first app launch or during active exam timer sessions, ensuring a smooth and uninterrupted learning experience.

For more details on how Google uses data from advertising partners, visit: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
